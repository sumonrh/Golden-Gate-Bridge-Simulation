Help and Assumptions

Interpretation: The bridge is scaled at 0.1 scene units per foot from published Golden Gate dimensions: 4,200 ft main span, 1,125 ft side spans, 746 ft towers above water, 220 ft roadway clearance, and a 90 ft deck. The main cables are seated at the 746 ft tower tops; the published 500 ft tower-above-roadway figure is a separate rounded reference datum. The 470 ft main-cable sag is a profile-based modeling assumption. The stiffening truss is modelled as dual 25 ft-deep (7.6 m) Warren trusses with verticals at 50 ft (15.24 m) hanger spacing — top/bottom chords, verticals, alternating diagonals, floor beams at each panel, and top/bottom lateral bracing (bottom X-bracing added 1953-54) — spaced 90 ft apart in the cable planes, with suspenders transferring deck load from floor-beam panel points to the main cables.

Hazards are deterministic teaching aids. Displayed deformation is not measured performance. The model includes below-water tower foundations but omits soil-structure interaction, aeroelastic stability, detailed traffic rules, local bathymetry, and certified material properties.
